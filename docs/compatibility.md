# Which Peterson tuners can use the OTB tunings

November 10, 2026. Three kinds of statement are kept apart below: what Peterson's own pages, manuals and support staff say; what I tested or infer; and what is still unknown.

The 20 OTB tunings are shared one at a time on Peterson's User Trading Post (Misc. Instruments, by Michael Marmor, since October 31, 2026). There is no single install of the whole set: the Trading Post does not yet allow whole configurations for the StroboClip HDC.

## The StroboClip HDC family

| Model | The 20 tunings | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| StroboClip HDC | Yes. The tunings were built on one, and I added one back from the Trading Post on November 10, 2026 (firmware 1.0.15). | Tested |
| StroboClip HDC-B | Yes. Peterson support: the HDC-B is the same tuner as the HDC with a different clip. | Peterson support, 29-Oct-2026 |
| StroboClip HDC-V | Very likely. Peterson's product page describes the same color display, 0.1-cent accuracy and per-tuning colors, with a wider clamp; Peterson support says the Standard, V-style and B-style clips fit any StroboClip HD, HDC, HDC-V or HDC-B. I infer the tunings load the same way. Not tested. | Peterson HDC-V page, fetched 29-Oct-2026; Peterson support, 27-Oct-2026; inference |

## Other Peterson Connect tuners

Peterson Connect, the browser tool that moves presets onto a tuner, works with these products (Peterson's Connect page, fetched 29-Oct-2026): StroboClip HDC, StroboStomp Mini, StroboPLUS HDC, StroboClip HD, StroboStomp HD, StroboStomp LE, StroboPLUS HD, and the Stomp Classic pedal. All of them accept custom sweetened tunings, so owners of these tuners can add the OTB tunings they want to their own list. I have not tested any of them.

What carries over to a single shared tuning:

| Feature | Which models | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The five target notes and the sweetener offsets | All eight Connect models | Peterson's Connect page: "Create your own sweetened tunings with custom offsets and note names" |
| Listening only for the tuning's five notes (a far-off string reads as the nearest of the five) | StroboClip HDC family (tested); StroboClip HD, StroboPLUS HD, Stomp Classic and StroboStomp HD (Peterson's Guided Tuning Manual, 2019); StroboStomp Mini, StroboStomp LE and StroboPLUS HDC: unknown | Guided Tuning Manual; tested on the HDC; the rest asked |
| A display color per tuning | The models with a color display: StroboClip HDC, HDC-B, HDC-V and StroboPLUS HDC. A shared tuning carries no color: on my HDC it arrived in the tuner's general color, and I set its color on the Configure page. | Peterson product pages; tested on the HDC, 10-Nov-2026 |
| The three-letter code | All models show a three-character preset name | Peterson manuals |
| The order on the tuner | A tuning is added to the bottom of your list; drag to reorder on the Configure page | Tested on the HDC, 10-Nov-2026 |

## The computer side

Peterson Connect runs in browsers that support WebHID: Google Chrome, Chromium, Microsoft Edge, Opera, Vivaldi and Comodo Dragon (Peterson's Connect page, fetched 29-Oct-2026). It does not work in Safari. Firefox is not on Peterson's list. You can design and configure in any browser; only the transfer to the tuner needs one of the listed browsers.

## Corrections

If you add the tunings to a model not covered above, or find a row here to be wrong, email marmor@duck.com or open an issue; the row will be updated with your model and firmware version.
